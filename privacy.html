Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

GymSensei is an iPhone app that recognises gym equipment from a photo and builds training plans around it. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to have it removed.

The app is published by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY], [YOUR COUNTRY]. For any privacy question, write to support@gymsensei.app.

The short version

  • You can use the app without an account.
  • Photos you scan are sent for analysis and are not stored by us or by the analysis provider.
  • We do not show ads, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or share your data for advertising.
  • You can ask us to delete everything, at any time.

If you never sign in, your data stays on your device and in an anonymous record that is not linked to your name or email.

What we do not collect

No advertising identifiers, no location, no contacts, no HealthKit data, no browsing activity, and no analytics about how you use the app.

Photos and the AI analysis

When you scan a machine, or use "Plan from a photo", that single image is sent over an encrypted connection to OpenRouter, which passes it to Google Gemini for analysis. What comes back is a short, text-only result: the names of the equipment recognised, or a list of muscle groups to prioritise.

The image is not saved by the app, is not written to our database, and is not retained by the providers after the request completes. It is not used to train any model.

The "Plan from a photo" feature returns training guidance only. It does not estimate body fat, weight, measurements or fitness level, and does not comment on your appearance.

Who else processes your data

  • Google (Firebase) — sign-in, database and server functions. Firebase privacy
  • OpenRouter and Google Gemini — image analysis, as described above. OpenRouter privacy
  • Cloudflare (R2) — serves the exercise videos and images. Standard server logs may record your IP address. Cloudflare privacy
  • Apple — handles all payments. We never see your card details. Apple privacy

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone beyond the providers above.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera — only to take a photo of equipment to scan. Nothing is recorded or stored.
  • Photo library — only to let you pick an existing photo to scan. The app sees only the photo you choose.

You can refuse or withdraw either permission in iOS Settings. The app still works; you will just use the other input method.

How long data is kept

Your account data is kept for as long as you use the app. If you delete the app without asking us to remove your data, the record stays until you ask for deletion. Photos are never kept.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, or delete all of it. Email support@gymsensei.app from the address linked to your account and we will act within 30 days, free of charge.

If you are in the EU, the UK or another region with equivalent rules, you also have the right to object to processing, to request a portable copy of your data, and to complain to your national data protection authority.

Children

GymSensei is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, email us and we will delete it.

Not medical advice

GymSensei provides general exercise information. It is not medical advice, not a diagnosis, and not a substitute for a qualified trainer or clinician. Train within your limits and seek professional advice if you are unsure about an exercise or have an injury or health condition.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, we will update the date at the top and, where the change is significant, tell you in the app.
