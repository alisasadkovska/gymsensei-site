How do I scan a machine?

Open the Scan tab, point your camera at the machine so most of it is in frame, and tap the shutter. You can also tap the photo icon on the left to pick an existing photo from your library.

Scans work best when the whole machine is visible and well lit. If the result looks wrong, step back and try again from a different angle.

A machine says "coming soon"

A handful of less common items don't have exercise content yet. We're adding them over time. If you hit one, email us the name and we'll prioritise it.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the Account tab and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with. Restoring works on any device.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, choose GymSensei, and tap Cancel. You keep PRO until the end of the period you've paid for.

Cancelling during a free trial stops any charge, as long as you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use the whole app without signing in. Signing in with Apple syncs your gyms, plans and workout history across your devices, and keeps them if you change phone.

Can I change the language?

Yes — Account tab → Language. GymSensei is available in English, Español, Français and Українська, independently of your phone's language.

The voice coach isn't speaking

Check Account → Coach voice isn't set to Off, and that your phone isn't in silent mode. Some languages have no male voice installed by default; you can add one in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.

How do I delete my data?

Email us from the address linked to your account and we'll delete everything we hold within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy for what's stored.

Is GymSensei a substitute for a trainer?

No. It gives general exercise information, not medical or professional advice. Train within your limits, and speak to a qualified professional if you're unsure about an exercise or have an injury.
